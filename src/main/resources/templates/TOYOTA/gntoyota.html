Toyota Gazoo Racing: Pushing the Limit

From the WRC to the street. Performance without compromise.

Toyota represents the "Street Performance" spirit of the CarNation collection, where Gazoo Racing (GR) transforms reliable daily drivers into track-ready beasts. With a focus on lightweight agility and turbocharged power, the GR series brings professional racing technology to enthusiasts everywhere.

The Gazoo Racing Lineup

The Legend

Toyota GR Supra

The return of an icon. 382 HP from a 3.0L twin-scroll turbocharged inline-six, delivering a perfect 50:50 weight balance for pure driving pleasure.

WRC DNA

Toyota GR Yaris

Born for the World Rally Championship. A lightweight AWD powerhouse with a turbocharged 1.6L engine that redefines the hot-hatch segment.

AWD Master

Toyota GR Corolla

300 HP and a highly customizable GR-FOUR all-wheel-drive system. It’s the ultimate practical performance car built for any road.

The Purist

Pure agility. A lightweight, rear-wheel-drive sports car that emphasizes handling and steering feedback over raw horsepower.
